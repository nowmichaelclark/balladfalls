# The playhead's designs (session 30k, item 119, step 1)

Four round playheads for Mikey to pick from, none with the sparkle, each
drawn on the card mirrored (art on the right, step 2). `proto.css` is the
sketch injected over the app for the screenshots; the sheet and an animated
version are on the branch `s30k-shots`. Only the one picked is built, and
this folder goes before the PR merges.

- **A, ring:** a solid ring with a dot in its middle, the theme's color, no glow.
- **B, bead:** a filled dot a size up, a thin gap ring and a soft shadow under it, like a knob.
- **C, trail:** a filled dot with the two steps just played behind it, fading.
- **D, beat pulse:** a filled dot; on each beat a ring widens from it and fades.

Round 2 (Mikey liked B, and asked whether it could look more like an LED):

- **B1, lens:** the bead lit from inside, with a lens's highlight; the gap ring and the shadow as B's, no glow.
- **B2, in a socket:** the same lens in a dark bezel, with a tight glow in its own color.
- **B3, diffused:** a hot middle out to its color, and a soft bloom round it.
