# docs/ears: what the ears sessions write

The reports, plan, album cards and raw data from the ears sessions. These
lived in the Procedural Music App Project until 2026-10-17; Mikey asked for
them in the repo, and for it to be a rule (the first section of
`sessions.md`): **ears outputs live in the repo.** The scripts they use are
in `tools/ears/`; the findings and yardsticks distilled for the brain and
hands are in `docs/EARS.md`.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `sessions.md` | Read first. What ears sessions are for, the repo rule, lessons, practical notes (Drive, pushing, piles). |
| `plan.md` | Every album on the list, the card format, the session split, costs. Read second. |
| `data.csv` | Raw per-track measurements for batches one and two and 104 Driftloom loops (`tools/ears/twins.py` reads it). |

**After 1.0 (2026-11-26, the slim):** the batch reports (`batch-1.md` to
`batch-6.md`), the album cards (`cards-*.md`), the session prompts, the
first sketches and the raw per-track archives (`raw/`, 21 MB) left the tree.
`docs/archived.txt` lists them and says where they still read. What they
found is on the Workshop Wiki's page "Ballad Falls/What the ears found"
(<https://nowmichaelclark.github.io/wiki/>), and the yardsticks stay in
`docs/EARS.md`. Whether ears sessions go on after 1.0, and where their new
outputs go, waits for Mikey.

New cards go in `cards-<pile>.md`, new batches in `batch-N.md`, in a PR from
the session that wrote them, until Mikey decides otherwise.
